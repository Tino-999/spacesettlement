About

This site is not a manifesto. It does not say space settlement is necessary. It does not say space settlement is the only path. This site is also not a wiki. It is not meant to be complete. It is meant to spark thinking. It curates solid, verifiable knowledge and puts it into context. The goal is to create a shared ground for a clear-headed discussion about whether a permanent human presence beyond Earth makes sense, is necessary, or is avoidable. This is about data, not opinions. About what is established and what is still unresolved. No persuasion. No future hype. Just the facts you need to ask the question seriously.
